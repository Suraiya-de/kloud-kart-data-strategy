Executive Summary
Kloud Kart's primary challenge is not the absence of advanced analytics capabilities, but the absence of a trusted and governed reporting environment.

Four root causes are creating operational inefficiency, compliance exposure, and direct business risk:

1. Reporting workloads compete with customer transactions.
2. Reporting metrics lack governance and a trusted source.
3. Customer data copies are unmanaged.
4. Reporting depends on manual spreadsheet processes.

Root Cause Analysis

Root Cause 1: Reporting and Customer Transactions Share thesame production Database
Evidence: Monday exports slowed the live website twice last month.
Why it matters: Reporting workloads compete directly with customer transactions.
Data Quality Dimensions: timeliness, availability
Cost Category: Direct Loss
Estimated Impact Any degradation of website responsiveness risks abandoned purchases, lost revenue and reduced customer confidence.

Root Cause 2: No trusted reporting or metric governance 
Evidence: Nana rebuilds numbers manually.
          Finance's spreadsheet never matches hers.
Why it matters: The company lacks a governed definition of key business metrics and operates without a trusted reporting source.
Data Quality Dimension: Consistency
Cost Category: wasted labour, risk exposure
Estimated Impact: Management decisions may be based on conflicting figures while significant staff time is consumed by reconciliation work.

Root Cause 3: Weak Governance of customer data copies
Evidence: Marketing exports customer lists.
          No tracking exists for copies.
Why it matters: Kloud Kart cannot confidently identify where customer data exists.
Data Quality Dimension: Integrity, Governance
Cost Category: Risk Weighted Exposure
Estimated Impact:This increases Act 843 compliance risk and directly relates to the ongoing Data Protection Commission complaint.

Root Cause 4: Reporting Processes Depends on Manual Spreadsheet Workflows
Evidence: Nana manually rebuilds Monday reporting.
Why it matters: Reporting quality depends on manual intervention rather than repeatable processes.
Data Quality Dimension: Accuracy, Completeness, Consistency
Cost Category: Wasted Labour
