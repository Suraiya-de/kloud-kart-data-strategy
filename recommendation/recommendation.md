
# Kloud Kart – Data Strategy Recommendation

## 1. Executive Recommendation

Kloud Kart should prioritise building a trusted reporting and governance foundation before investing in advanced analytics capabilities.

Over the next twelve months, the company should focus on:

1. Separating reporting workloads from the live website.
2. Establishing a single trusted source for business reporting.
3. Reducing manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
4. Improving control over customer data.

This approach addresses the highest operational and compliance risks currently facing the company while remaining realistic for a team that can only hire two data specialists this financial year.

The recommendation intentionally favours operational stability, reporting trust, and regulatory compliance over complex technologies. Real-time analytics, machine learning forecasting, and more advanced data platforms should be treated as later-stage investments after the reporting environment becomes trusted, repeatable, and governed.

## 2. Why This Recommendation Fits Kloud Kart

### 2.1 Team Size and Skills

Kloud Kart currently has one PHP/MySQL developer and no established data team. The company is approved to hire only two data professionals this financial year.

Any recommendation must therefore minimise operational complexity and avoid creating systems that require significant specialist expertise to maintain.

The proposed approach focuses on capabilities that can be operated successfully by a small team while still improving reporting reliability and supporting business growth.

### 2.2 Operational Risk

The most immediate operational risk is that reporting workloads currently run against the same environment that supports customer transactions.

The brief confirms that Monday exports slowed the live website twice during the previous month.

Separating reporting activity from operational activity reduces the risk of reporting impacting customer purchases and protects revenue-generating business processes.

### 2.3 Data Quality and Reporting

Nana currently rebuilds reporting figures manually, and Finance's spreadsheet does not match hers.

This indicates a lack of trusted reporting processes and suggests that key business metrics are not consistently governed.

The recommendation prioritises creating a trusted reporting environment so that operational and financial reporting can be produced from the same source using agreed definitions.

### 2.4 Compliance and Act 843

Marketing exports customer lists without tracking how many copies exist. The company is also responding to a Data Protection Commission complaint involving customer data.

The recommendation therefore prioritises governance, visibility of customer-data copies, and repeatable data management practices before expanding the number of systems that hold customer information.

### 2.5 Growth and Expansion

Kloud Kart is growing steadily and has begun a Nigeria pilot.

The proposed approach supports near-term expansion while avoiding investment in infrastructure designed for much larger organisations.

The recommendation focuses on solving today's problems while creating a foundation that can support future growth.

## 3. What We Are Not Doing Yet

### 3.1 Real-Time Analytics

Real-time analytics is not recommended as an immediate priority.

The brief identifies stock freshness as the only process that requires near-real-time updates. Most reporting requirements can tolerate daily refreshes.

The additional cost and operational complexity of a broader streaming architecture cannot currently be justified by the business need.

### 3.2 Machine Learning for Demand Forecasting

Machine learning forecasting is not recommended during the first year.

Reliable forecasting depends upon trusted, consistent historical data.

Current effort should focus on improving reporting quality and governance before introducing advanced analytical workloads.

### 3.3 Lakehouse Architecture

A lakehouse architecture is not recommended as an immediate investment.

While it may support future analytical and machine-learning requirements, the current business challenges relate to reporting reliability, governance, and operational stability rather than limitations of the existing analytical platform.

## 4. Expected Outcomes After 12 Months

By the end of the first twelve months, Kloud Kart should achieve the following outcomes:

1. Reporting workloads no longer negatively affect the live website.
2. Finance and Operations work from the same trusted reporting source.
3. Manual reconciliation effort is substantially reduced.
4. Customer-data copies are identified, governed, and easier to manage under Act 843 obligations.
5. Board reporting is repeatable and based on agreed business definitions.
6. Kloud Kart has established a realistic foundation for future forecasting and analytical initiatives.

## 5. Assumptions

The recommendation is based on the following assumptions:

### Assumption 1
The board can accept daily data refreshes for reporting workloads outside inventory management.

### Assumption 2
Inventory freshness remains the only business process requiring near-real-time updates.

### Assumption 3
Order volume continues to grow at approximately the rate described in the brief and does not experience an extraordinary increase requiring a fundamentally different architecture.

### Assumption 4
The company intends to respond to the current Data Protection Commission complaint and improve customer-data governance during the next financial year.

### Assumption 5
The budget remains limited to the two approved data hires and does not expand significantly during the roadmap period.
