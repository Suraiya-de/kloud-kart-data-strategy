
# Nana's Monday Reporting Pack

## 1. Overview

Nana's Monday Reporting Pack is a standardised weekly reporting tool proposed as an improvement beyond the original Kloud Kart Data Strategy Case Study brief.

It is designed to help Nana Adjei, Head of Operations, prepare, review and communicate weekly business performance figures more consistently.

The improvement addresses the manual effort involved in rebuilding Monday reports from CSV exports and the inconsistencies between Finance and Operations reporting figures.

## 2. Problem Being Addressed

Kloud Kart currently experiences challenges with its weekly reporting process:

- Nana manually rebuilds Monday reports using CSV exports.
- Finance and Operations sometimes produce different figures.
- Reporting figures are not consistently defined across departments.
- Missing, duplicate or inconsistent records can affect reporting accuracy.
- Preparing and reconciling reports takes time that could otherwise be spent on operational analysis.

## 3. Proposed Improvement

The proposed improvement is a standardised Monday Reporting Pack containing:

1. **Weekly reporting template:** A consistent layout for presenting total orders, total sales, revenue, average order value and reporting-period information.
2. **KPI definitions:** Clear explanations of each reported measure and how it should be calculated.
3. **Data quality checks:** Simple checks for missing values, duplicate records, invalid dates and incomplete reporting periods.
4. **Reconciliation log:** A structured way to compare Finance and Operations figures, document differences and track their investigation.

The initial demonstration will use illustrative sample data rather than actual Kloud Kart customer or transaction records.

## 4. Value to Nana and the Business

The improvement is primarily intended to reduce the manual effort involved in preparing weekly reports and make reporting more consistent.

Its expected value includes:

- **Less repetitive preparation:** Nana receives a consistent reporting structure instead of rebuilding the report from scratch each week.
- **Easier discrepancy investigation:** Differences between Finance and Operations figures can be recorded and investigated systematically.
- **Greater clarity:** Clearly defined KPIs help stakeholders understand what the reported figures represent.
- **Better visibility of data quality:** Basic checks help identify incomplete or inconsistent information before reports are shared.
- **More time for operational decisions:** Nana can spend less time organising figures and more time interpreting business performance.

This improvement supports more reliable communication between Operations and Finance and provides a repeatable foundation for weekly business reporting.

## 5. Scope and Limitations

The initial version is a reporting support tool and will use illustrative data for demonstration.

It will not connect directly to Kloud Kart's production database or access actual customer information.

In a future implementation, the reporting pack could be populated from the proposed governed reporting environment.

Operational inventory decisions must continue to rely on current live inventory data rather than a potentially delayed reporting copy.

## 6. Success Criteria

The improvement will be considered successfully demonstrated when:

- A weekly reporting template is available.
- The selected KPIs have clear definitions.
- The sample data can be used to demonstrate the reporting process.
- Basic data quality issues can be identified.
- Finance and Operations differences can be documented and investigated.
- Nana can follow the reporting process using the accompanying instructions.

## 7. Status

**Status: Proposed for implementation.**

The reporting pack is intended to be the practical improvement beyond the original case study deliverables.
